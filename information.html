Fondation Tschuess

Mara Wohnhaas, Matthias Holznagel and Hagen Eberle—alias Fondation Tschuess—operate on a case-by-case basis in continually shifting circumstances. Central to FT's practice is the continuous reflection and observation of exhibitionmaking.

Since 2023 works by Sarah Albrecht, Amy Balkin, Dirk Bell, Jb Burguet, Ernst Caramelle, Juliet Carpenter, Elisa Diaferia, Pia-Rosa Dobrowitz, Hagen Eberle, Jessie Evans, Loretta Fahrenholz, Harun Farocki, Silke Fehsenfeld, Yaël Fidesser, Simon Fischer, Heike-Karin Föll, Magdalena Frauenberg, Boaz Yosef Friedman, Katrin Fuchsloch, Juri Groß, Louis Hay, Florian Hofer, Sam Holzberg, Matthias Holznagel, James T. Hong, Danièle Huillet & Jean-Marie Straub, Gvantsa Jgushia, Rafael Jörger, Zoe Agnes Jungbluth, Morag Keil, Klasse John Bock, Tamara Knežević, Anne Korth, Jonida Laçi, Sophie Lindt, Devrim Lingnau, Jan-Hendrik Linßen, Sam Marshall Lockyer, Úlfur Loga, Sam Mackiewicz, Kofi Møller, Lukas Müller, Philip Nürnberger, Simon Persson, Aaron Pfersdorf, F Pilgram, Leni Pohl, Carla Luisa Reuter, Veronika Saly, Helene von Schirach, Miriam Schmitz, Ben Schumacher, Monika Schwitte, Manuel Sékou, Stella Sieber, Linda Skellington, Cally Spooner, Silke Stock, Mia Trautmann, Lukas Vögele, Helen Weber, Alex Winterstein, Mara Wohnhaas, Constanze Zacharias und Hazel Meggie Zander have been conceived and shown.
